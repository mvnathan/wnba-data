Chronological holdout performance

Performance is measured on the newest 20% of historical matches, after training on the earlier 80%. Spread error is measured against final game differential; total error is measured against total games played.

Model design

Separate ATP and WTA models use the most recent 730 days of completed professional matches where at least one player was ranked No. 150 or better. Inputs include rank and ranking points, overall and surface Elo, recent win rate, recent game differential, serve-point form, experience, surface, and match format. Winner, spread, and total come from separate gradient-boosted models. Market odds are not model inputs.
The spread is Player 1 games minus Player 2 games. Positive values favor Player 1; negative values favor Player 2. Predictions are estimates, not guarantees.
