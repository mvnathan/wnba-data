Model design

Targets: winner probability, scoring margin, and game total. Candidate logistic, gradient-boosted, random-forest, extra-trees, and ridge models compete on a chronological validation slice; the final 20% remains untouched until evaluation. Inputs include 10-game possession-adjusted offensive and defensive ratings, pace, eFG%, turnover rate, offensive-rebound rate, free-throw rate, recent margin volatility, rest, roster continuity, incoming-player minute share, prior player value, and top-three rotation concentration. Historical roster features use only information available before each game. Trusted X availability adjustments are applied only when a current-roster player is explicitly matched, and the adjustment is capped and weighted by rotation share and source confidence.
